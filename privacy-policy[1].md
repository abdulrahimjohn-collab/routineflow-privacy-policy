# RoutineFlow Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 2, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to RoutineFlow. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the RoutineFlow application.

## 2. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use RoutineFlow, the app may handle:

**Account information**
- Email address.
- Name and basic profile information made available when you sign in with Google.
- Authentication information needed to create and manage your account.

**Information you enter in the app**
- Tasks.
- Daily routines and activities.
- Calendar and scheduling information.
- App preferences and settings.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:
- Create and manage your account.
- Allow sign-in using Google or email and password.
- Save and retrieve tasks, routines and calendar information.
- Provide app features and notifications.
- Maintain and improve the app.
- Respond to support and account-deletion requests.

## 4. Data Storage and Service Providers

RoutineFlow uses Google Firebase services, including:

- **Firebase Authentication** to manage account registration and sign-in.
- **Cloud Firestore** to store and retrieve information associated with user accounts, such as tasks and routines.

Google processes information in connection with the services it provides, subject to its applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Advertising and Analytics

RoutineFlow does not currently display in-app advertisements. If advertising or analytics services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

## 6. Data Sharing

RoutineFlow does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Google/Firebase as needed to provide the app's account, storage and related services. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

## 7. Logging Out

Users can log out of RoutineFlow at any time. Logging out does not itself delete the account or permanently erase saved tasks and routines. When a user signs in again with the same account, previously saved information may become available again.

## 8. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your RoutineFlow account and associated personal information by emailing:

**payet3john@gmail.com**

Removing RoutineFlow from your Google connected apps is not the same as deleting your RoutineFlow account or its stored data.

## 9. Data Security

RoutineFlow uses services and security features provided by Firebase and Google to help protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Data Retention

Account information and associated app data are retained for as long as needed to provide the service, or until a verified deletion request is processed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 11. Children's Privacy

RoutineFlow is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RoutineFlow's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same privacy policy address.
